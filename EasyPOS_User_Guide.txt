EASYPOS 1.4 - COMPLETE USER GUIDE
Developed by HamdhyTech
Android package: com.hamdhytech.easypos

======================================================================
1. ABOUT EASYPOS
======================================================================

EasyPOS is a lightweight Android Point of Sale application designed for
small shops and businesses. The main application works offline and stores
business information, products, stock, sales, settings, and reports in a
local SQLite database on the device.

Default currency:

  Currency code: LKR
  Currency symbol: Rs.
  Decimal places: 2
  Example: Rs. 1,250.00

Main features:

  - Offline product and stock management
  - Barcode scanning and internal barcode generation
  - Quantity confirmation before adding products to a sale
  - Cash, card, bank transfer, mobile, credit, and other payments
  - Automatic stock deduction
  - Sales history and sale cancellation
  - PDF e-bills and bill QR codes
  - Reports and CSV export
  - Complete backup and restore
  - Optional administrator PIN and biometric authentication
  - Light, dark, and system themes

======================================================================
2. INSTALLING EASYPOS
======================================================================

1. Copy the EasyPOS APK to the Android phone.
2. Open the APK from Files, Downloads, WhatsApp, Drive, or another source.
3. If Android displays an installation warning, allow installation from
   that source in the phone settings.
4. Select Install.
5. Open EasyPOS after installation.
6. Grant camera permission when barcode scanning or product photography
   is used for the first time.

Important:

  - Download APKs only from the official HamdhyTech source.
  - Before updating or uninstalling, create a complete backup.
  - Installing a newer EasyPOS version over the existing application
    normally keeps the local database.
  - Uninstalling the application can erase all locally stored data.

======================================================================
3. FIRST-TIME BUSINESS SETUP
======================================================================

When EasyPOS opens for the first time:

1. Enter the business name.
2. Enter the owner's name.
3. Enter the phone number if required.
4. Enter the business address if required.
5. Confirm LKR as the currency.
6. Optionally create a four-digit administrator PIN.
7. Select Start using EasyPOS.

The administrator PIN protects sensitive areas such as Settings and
Reports. Remember the PIN. EasyPOS does not send it to a server.

Two sample products may be available in a new database. They can be
edited or removed if they have not been used in a sale.

======================================================================
4. HOME DASHBOARD
======================================================================

The Home screen provides a summary of the business:

  - Today's sales value
  - Number of bills today
  - Number of low-stock products
  - Total number of products

Quick actions provide access to:

  - New Sale
  - Products
  - Stock
  - Sales History
  - Barcodes
  - Reports

Pull down on the dashboard to refresh product and dashboard information.
The dashboard also refreshes automatically after product, stock, and sale
changes.

======================================================================
5. ADDING A PRODUCT
======================================================================

1. Open Products from the bottom navigation.
2. Select the full-width Add Product button.
3. Enter the product name.
4. Enter or scan the barcode. A barcode is optional, but recommended.
5. Enter the brand if required.
6. Select a category.
7. Enter the cost price.
8. Enter the selling price.
9. Enter the opening stock.
10. Enter the low-stock warning level.
11. Select the unit:

    - Pieces
    - Kilograms
    - Grams
    - Litres
    - Millilitres

12. Enter the tax percentage if required.
13. Add notes if required.
14. Optionally capture a product photograph.
15. Optionally set an expiry date.
16. Enable Quick Sale favourite if the product is commonly sold.
17. Select Save Product.

If the barcode already exists, EasyPOS asks whether the existing product
should be updated. Review the details carefully before confirming.

======================================================================
6. EDITING OR REMOVING PRODUCTS
======================================================================

1. Open Products.
2. Search by product name or barcode.
3. Open the required product.
4. Select Edit to change its information.
5. Save the changes.

A product that has already been used in a sale cannot be permanently
deleted because it is required for sales records. Update or retain the
product to preserve accurate history.

======================================================================
7. CREATING INTERNAL BARCODES
======================================================================

EasyPOS creates internal Code 128 barcodes for products. These are suitable
for internal shop use. They are not registered UPC or EAN retail numbers.

To create a barcode for a new product:

1. Open Barcodes from the Home screen.
2. Select Create a New Product Barcode.
3. Enter the product name.
4. Enter the selling price.
5. Select Generate.
6. EasyPOS creates an internal barcode number.
7. Add the remaining product information.
8. Save the product.
9. Select the number of barcode-label copies required.
10. Choose the PDF layout:

    - One large label
    - Two columns
    - Three columns

11. Select Generate and Share PDF.
12. Save, share, or print the PDF labels.

To create labels for existing products, use Add Product inside the Barcode
Creator and select products that already have barcode numbers.

======================================================================
8. MAKING A SALE
======================================================================

1. Open Sale from the bottom navigation.
2. Search for a product or scan its barcode.
3. When a product is selected, EasyPOS asks for the quantity.
4. Quantity defaults to 1.
5. Change the quantity if required.
6. Select Add to Cart.
7. Repeat for all products.
8. Select View Cart.
9. Review products, quantities, tax, discount, and total.
10. Optionally enter the customer name and phone number.
11. Select the payment method.
12. For cash, enter the amount received.
13. For credit, enter any initial payment.
14. Select Complete Sale.

EasyPOS validates stock before completing the transaction. Unless negative
stock is enabled in Settings, quantities above the available stock cannot
be sold.

After successful payment:

  - A sequential bill number is generated.
  - Stock is deducted automatically.
  - The sale appears in Sales History.
  - The dashboard is updated.
  - The bill can be shared as a PDF.
  - A bill QR code can be displayed.

======================================================================
9. SCANNING A BARCODE DURING A SALE
======================================================================

1. Open Sale.
2. Select the scanner button.
3. Allow camera permission if requested.
4. Place the barcode inside the scanning frame.
5. If the product exists, enter the quantity and select Add to Cart.
6. If the product is unknown, EasyPOS may search Open Food Facts when
   online lookup is enabled.
7. Add the unknown product, enter local price and stock information, save
   it, and then confirm the sale quantity.

Barcode scanning works with the phone camera. Use good lighting and keep
the barcode flat and fully visible.

A successful scan produces sound and haptic feedback. The result depends on
the phone's media/system volume and vibration settings.

======================================================================
10. SHOPPING CART AND STOCK VALIDATION
======================================================================

Inside the cart:

  - Use plus to increase quantity.
  - Use minus to reduce quantity.
  - Use delete to remove an item.
  - Enter a discount if required.
  - Review subtotal, tax, discount, total, and cash balance.

If a requested quantity is higher than available stock, EasyPOS displays
the available amount and prevents the invalid addition. Negative stock can
be permitted from Settings when the business requires it.

======================================================================
11. PAYMENT METHODS
======================================================================

EasyPOS supports:

  - Cash
  - Card
  - Bank transfer
  - Mobile payment
  - Credit
  - Other

Cash:

  Enter the amount received. EasyPOS shows the customer balance.

Credit:

  Enter an optional initial payment. The unpaid amount is recorded as the
  bill balance. Additional payments can be recorded later from Sales
  History.

Other electronic methods:

  EasyPOS records the selected method and treats the bill as paid. The
  actual bank, card, or mobile transaction is processed outside EasyPOS.

======================================================================
12. SALES HISTORY
======================================================================

1. Open Sales from the bottom navigation.
2. Search using the bill number or customer name.
3. Select a bill to view:

   - Products and quantities
   - Unit prices
   - Subtotal
   - Discount
   - Tax
   - Total
   - Payment information

Available bill actions:

  - Download or share the e-bill
  - Show the bill QR
  - Record a credit payment
  - Cancel the sale and return stock

Sales History refreshes automatically after checkout, payment, restore, or
sale cancellation. Pull down on the list if a manual refresh is required.

======================================================================
13. CANCELLING A SALE
======================================================================

1. Open Sales History.
2. Open the required completed bill.
3. Select Cancel and Return Stock.
4. Confirm the cancellation.

EasyPOS marks the sale as cancelled and returns the sold quantities to
stock. The original record remains available for audit purposes. Only
completed, non-cancelled sales can be cancelled.

======================================================================
14. CREDIT PAYMENTS
======================================================================

1. Open a credit bill with an outstanding balance.
2. Select Record Credit Payment.
3. Enter the amount received.
4. Save the payment.

The payment cannot be greater than the outstanding balance. When the
balance reaches zero, the bill is marked paid.

======================================================================
15. PDF E-BILLS
======================================================================

After a sale or from Sales History:

1. Select Share Bill or Download or Share E-Bill.
2. Android displays the available applications.
3. Save the PDF, send it to the customer, upload it to cloud storage, or
   open it with a compatible printing application.

The receipt contains:

  - EasyPOS and business information
  - Bill number and date
  - Customer name when supplied
  - Items, quantities, prices, and totals
  - Payment details
  - Bill QR code
  - Configurable bill footer

======================================================================
16. BILL QR CODES
======================================================================

To show a QR immediately after payment:

1. Complete the sale.
2. Select Show QR.

To show a QR later:

1. Open Sales History.
2. Open the bill.
3. Select Show Bill QR.

The QR contains a compact offline bill reference including the business,
bill number, date, total, payment method, and payment status. It is not an
internet webpage because EasyPOS does not require a hosted cloud server.

Use a standard QR scanner to read the bill summary. Increase screen
brightness and avoid reflections if another phone has difficulty scanning.

======================================================================
17. STOCK MANAGEMENT
======================================================================

Open Stock from the Home screen to review available quantities and
low-stock products.

To adjust stock:

1. Find or scan the product.
2. Open the stock adjustment action.
3. Select stock in or stock out.
4. Enter the quantity.
5. Enter the reason.
6. Confirm the adjustment.

EasyPOS records stock movements with previous quantity, new quantity,
reason, reference, date, and actor. Stock cannot be manually adjusted below
zero.

The Products screen also provides a full-width Scan Barcode button directly
below Add Product. Use it to open an existing product or create a new product
with the scanned barcode already filled in.

======================================================================
18. REPORTS
======================================================================

Open Reports from the Home screen. Administrator authentication may be
required.

Reports include:

  - Sales overview
  - Daily sales
  - Profit estimate
  - Best-selling products
  - Sales by payment method

Reports use completed sales and exclude cancelled bills where applicable.
CSV export can be shared or saved using Android's share options.

Profit is an estimate based on recorded cost price, selling price, quantity,
and discounts. Accurate product cost prices are required for meaningful
profit reporting.

======================================================================
19. SETTINGS
======================================================================

Open Settings from the bottom navigation. Enter the administrator PIN or
use biometric authentication when configured.

Business:

  Edit the business name, owner, phone number, and address.

Currency:

  LKR and Rs. are selected by default. Other available currencies can be
  selected when required.

Theme:

  - System
  - Light
  - Dark

Sales:

  - Allow or prevent negative stock
  - Enable or disable online product lookup
  - Edit the bill footer
  - Edit the bill-number prefix

Security:

  - Change or remove the four-digit administrator PIN
  - Enable biometric authentication when supported by the phone

Data:

  - Upload or share complete backup
  - Download or restore backup
  - Reset all application data

======================================================================
20. COMPLETE BACKUP
======================================================================

The complete backup contains:

  - Business configuration
  - Categories
  - Products
  - Stock quantities
  - Customers stored with sales
  - Sales and sale items
  - Payments
  - Stock movements
  - Barcode batch history
  - Application settings

To create and upload a backup:

1. Open Settings.
2. Select Upload or Share Complete Backup.
3. Select Google Drive, OneDrive, Files, email, or another safe location.
4. Wait until the destination confirms the upload.

Create backups regularly and before:

  - Updating EasyPOS
  - Changing phones
  - Resetting the application
  - Uninstalling the application
  - Sending the phone for repair

The automatic local backup is not a replacement for copying a backup away
from the phone. A lost or damaged phone can also lose its local backups.

======================================================================
21. RESTORING ON A NEW DEVICE
======================================================================

1. Install EasyPOS on the new Android device.
2. Download the EasyPOS .db backup from the chosen cloud location.
3. Open EasyPOS and complete temporary first-time setup if required.
4. Open Settings.
5. Select Download or Restore Backup.
6. Enter the administrator PIN if requested.
7. Select the downloaded .db file.
8. Confirm Restore.
9. Wait for the confirmation message.
10. Review Products, Stock, Sales History, and Settings.

Restore replaces the current local database. EasyPOS creates a fresh backup
of the current database before replacement.

Product photographs stored as local file paths may not be available on the
new phone. Core product, stock, sale, and configuration information is
stored in the database backup.

======================================================================
22. ADMINISTRATOR PIN AND BIOMETRICS
======================================================================

The administrator PIN protects sensitive functions.

To change the PIN:

1. Open Settings.
2. Select Change Admin PIN.
3. Authenticate with the current PIN if required.
4. Enter a new four-digit PIN.
5. Save.

To remove the PIN, leave the new PIN field blank when the option is
available.

To enable biometrics:

1. Configure fingerprint or face authentication in Android settings.
2. Open EasyPOS Settings.
3. Enable Biometric Authentication.

If biometrics are unavailable, the PIN remains available.

======================================================================
23. RESETTING EASYPOS
======================================================================

Reset permanently removes products, sales, stock, business details, and
settings from the active database.

1. Create and verify an external backup.
2. Open Settings.
3. Select Reset Application Data.
4. Authenticate.
5. Type RESET exactly.
6. Confirm.

EasyPOS creates a local backup before resetting, but an external copy is
strongly recommended.

======================================================================
24. UPDATING EASYPOS
======================================================================

1. Create a complete backup.
2. Download the newer official APK.
3. Install it over the existing EasyPOS application.
4. Do not uninstall the old application first.
5. Open EasyPOS.
6. Verify products, stock, settings, and Sales History.

Android may refuse an update if the APK uses a different signing key. If
that happens, do not uninstall until a valid external backup has been
created and verified.

25. TROUBLESHOOTING
======================================================================

Application does not install:

  - Allow installation from the APK source.
  - Confirm that the phone uses Android 7.0/API 24 or later.
  - Confirm that sufficient storage is available.

Scanner does not open:

  - Grant camera permission in Android Settings > Apps > EasyPOS.
  - Close other applications using the camera.
  - Restart EasyPOS.

Barcode is not detected:

  - Improve lighting.
  - Clean the camera lens.
  - Keep the barcode flat.
  - Move the phone closer or farther away.
  - Enter the barcode manually if necessary.

QR is difficult to scan:

  - Increase screen brightness.
  - Remove glare.
  - Hold the scanning phone steady.
  - Open the QR from Sales History again.

Product will not enter the cart:

  - Enter a quantity greater than zero.
  - Check available stock.
  - Reduce the requested quantity or adjust stock.
  - Enable negative stock only when appropriate.

Sale cannot be completed:

  - Confirm that the cart is not empty.
  - Check stock quantities.
  - For cash, enter an amount equal to or greater than the total.
  - Check that discount and payment values are valid numbers.

Sales History appears empty:

  - Clear the search box.
  - Pull down to refresh.
  - Confirm that the sale completed successfully.
  - Check whether a restored backup contains sales.

Backup cannot be restored:

  - Select an EasyPOS .db backup.
  - Download the file fully before selecting it.
  - Confirm that the file is not damaged or renamed incorrectly.
  - Keep the existing data until a valid backup is available.

Administrator PIN is rejected:

  - Enter exactly four digits.
  - Confirm the correct business backup is active.
  - Try configured biometric authentication.

Application displays a red Flutter error screen:

  - Record the exact actions that caused it.
  - Take a screenshot.
  - Restart EasyPOS and check whether the operation was saved.
  - Report the EasyPOS version, Android model, and steps to HamdhyTech.

======================================================================
27. DATA SAFETY
======================================================================

EasyPOS is offline-first. The business owner is responsible for protecting
the device and backups.

Recommended precautions:

  - Use an Android screen lock.
  - Set an EasyPOS administrator PIN.
  - Enable biometrics when appropriate.
  - Create frequent external backups.
  - Keep more than one recent backup.
  - Do not share database backups publicly.
  - Verify the destination before sending bills or reports.
  - Do not uninstall EasyPOS without a verified backup.
